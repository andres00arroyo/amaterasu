FAQ

My history disappeared / the app says history is unavailable

Your history lives only on your device. If the app was reinstalled or its data was cleared, past sessions can't be recovered. If you see a "history unavailable" message on launch, contact support with your iPhone model and iOS version.

Can I share my progress?

Yes — after a session, tap Share progress on the completion screen to create a shareable image. Nothing is uploaded; the image is created on your device.

Is breathwork safe for me?

Amaterasu includes a safety guide on first launch (replayable anytime from Settings). Practice seated or lying down, never while driving or in water, and stop if you feel dizzy or unwell. The app is a wellness tool, not medical advice — consult a healthcare professional if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, epilepsy, or are pregnant.

Does the app need the internet?

No. Amaterasu works fully offline — all guidance audio and session data are on your device.
