01 / BREATHE30 BREATHS

Find your rhythm.

Follow thirty guided inhales and exhales as your sun takes shape, one blade at a time.

02 / HOLDYOUR PACE

Rest in the quiet.

After the final exhale, hold only as long as feels comfortable. The eclipse marks this quiet interval.

03 / RECOVER15 SECONDS

Let the light return.

A guided recovery inhale and fifteen-second hold complete the round. Breathe sooner whenever you need to.

05 / A FEW THINGS

Before
you begin.

A little clarity for your first practice.

Is Amaterasu really free?
Yes. The current app is free to download and use, with no subscription, ads, or account required.
What kind of breathwork is this?
Each round combines thirty guided breaths, a comfortable exhaled retention, and a fifteen-second recovery hold. Choose one to five rounds. Amaterasu is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Wim Hof Method.
How long is a session?
A three-round session is estimated at roughly ten minutes, plus introductory guidance. Your actual time depends on the number of rounds and how long your comfortable holds last. Choose a single round when you want a shorter practice.
Can I use it without internet?
Yes. Once downloaded, the guidance, music, visuals, and local session history work offline. You don’t need an account.
Is this practice right for me?
This practice can cause lightheadedness or loss of consciousness. Consult a licensed medical professional to confirm it is appropriate for you. Practice seated or lying down somewhere stable, never in or near water, while driving, at heights, or around machinery. Never force a hold; stop immediately if you feel unwell. The app includes a safety guide before your first session.
Which devices does it support?
Amaterasu is designed for iPhone running iOS 17 or later. It uses your iPhone’s audio and haptics to guide the practice.

Make room for a safe practice. Always sit or lie down. Never practice in or near water, while driving, or anywhere a loss of consciousness could cause harm. Never force your breath. Read the safety guide before beginning.
